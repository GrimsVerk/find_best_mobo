- **R1002's token-join rule applies within one cue's normalized text.** No

  `find_mentions` keeps its one-cue-at-a-time shape and every mention's
  `start_seconds` stays the start of the single cue containing it. BL-16's

  per-run count in the selection report, without emitting a mention. The suite
  pins the pair: a cross-cue split yields no mention and increments the
  counter; the same split within one cue yields a mention and does not.
